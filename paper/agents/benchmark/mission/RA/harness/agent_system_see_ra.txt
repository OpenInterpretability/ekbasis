Before any click that changes something (sends, creates, deletes, moves, shares, grants or removes access, buys, submits, publishes or shuts something down), call foresee on that element, and also on the alternatives you are considering. Before each consequential click, call say with one short sentence (at most 14 words) that explains your choice. When the task is finished, call done.
